# Problem-Solving Document

## Project Title

**Smart Restaurant Experience Platform**

---

# Problem Statement

Restaurants in Rwanda struggle to collect customer feedback, manage complaints, maintain updated menus, and build an online presence. As a result, managers lack reliable data, customers feel unheard, menus become outdated, and restaurants remain difficult to discover.

---

# Objective

Design a digital platform that enables restaurants to gather feedback, manage operations, update menus quickly, and reach customers through a searchable directory.

---

# Problem Analysis

## Problem 1: Customers Do Not Share Feedback

### Current Situation

Most dissatisfied customers leave the restaurant without informing management.

### Impact

* Customer churn
* Negative word-of-mouth
* Lost revenue
* Repeated service mistakes

### Proposed Solution

Provide a simple QR-code-based review form that customers can complete in under one minute, without creating an account.

---

## Problem 2: Managers Lack Operational Insights

### Current Situation

Managers rely on verbal reports or assumptions.

### Impact

* Difficult to identify recurring issues
* Inability to measure customer satisfaction
* Poor decision-making

### Proposed Solution

Offer dashboards with ratings, trends, complaint categories, and AI-generated summaries.

---

## Problem 3: Printed Menus Are Costly and Slow to Update

### Current Situation

Menu updates require reprinting.

### Impact

* High recurring costs
* Outdated prices
* Operational delays

### Proposed Solution

Provide digital menus editable from the dashboard, with changes reflected instantly through the restaurant's QR code.

---

## Problem 4: Restaurants Have Limited Online Presence

### Current Situation

Many restaurants depend only on Instagram or WhatsApp.

### Impact

* Low discoverability
* Reduced customer reach
* Lack of centralized information

### Proposed Solution

Create a searchable public restaurant directory featuring menus, locations, ratings, galleries, and contact information.

---

## Problem 5: Employee Performance Is Difficult to Measure

### Current Situation

Managers rely on observation and occasional complaints.

### Impact

* Good staff go unrecognized
* Poor performance persists

### Proposed Solution

Aggregate customer feedback linked to staff (when provided) to generate performance metrics, while ensuring reviews are interpreted responsibly and not used as the sole basis for employment decisions.

---

# Proposed Solution Overview

The platform will combine the following components:

* QR-code-based customer feedback and complaint forms
* Editable digital menus
* Restaurant profiles in a public directory
* Analytics dashboards for operational insights
* Employee performance tracking linked to feedback

# Risks and Mitigation

| Risk                        | Mitigation                                                                                              |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Restaurants hesitate to pay | Offer a free tier and demonstrate ROI through analytics and reduced costs.                              |
| Low customer participation  | Keep reviews short, mobile-friendly, and accessible via QR codes.                                       |
| Fake or malicious reviews   | Implement moderation, rate limiting, optional receipt verification, and anomaly detection.              |
| Slow adoption               | Start with pilot restaurants and build case studies before scaling.                                     |
| Competition                 | Differentiate through operational tools, AI insights, and local market focus rather than just QR menus. |
